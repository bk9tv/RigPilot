# RigPilot – Logo

Originales R-Monogramm mit Startpfeil im Negativraum und separatem petrolfarbenem Rennstreifen. Die Mint-/Petrolfarben passen zur bestehenden dunklen Oberfläche.

Erzeugung: integriertes Imagegen-Werkzeug, kein API-/CLI-Fallback. Die PNG ist die ausgewählte Bilddatei mit Alpha; UI-PNG und ICO sind daraus unverändert skalierte Formatvarianten.

- `rigpilot-logo.png`: transparente Masterdatei.
- `rigpilot-ui.png`: 384 Pixel für die App-Oberfläche.
- `rigpilot.ico`: Windows-Symbol mit 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 und 256 Pixeln.
- `prepare-icons.ps1` im Projektverzeichnis erzeugt die Formatvarianten erneut.

## Generierungsprompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final standalone app logo for RigPilot, a refined Windows 11 control center for simracing / gaming, startup groups, audio and monitors. The existing interface is dark midnight navy (#0E131B) with mint green (#81E0B6). Design a memorable, bold geometric R monogram with a forward-moving racing apex / play-arrow cutout in its negative space. Confident motorsport-tech feeling, beautifully balanced, compact near-square silhouette, thick continuous forms and a very clear outline so it works as a 24px Windows taskbar icon. Flat vector-like precision, mint green with a restrained teal accent, exceptionally clean edges. Single centered logo mark, about 82 percent of the square canvas, transparent background with actual alpha. No text, no wordmark, no letters besides the abstract R, no slogan, no presentation board, no mockup, no extra symbols, no bevel, no shadow, no glow, no texture. Produce a single polished production-quality logo, 1024 x 1024.

## Verfeinerungsprompt

Refine this RigPilot logo for final production. Preserve the exact R silhouette, forward play-arrow negative space, and the separated teal lower-left stroke. Clean up ALL stray colored flecks, dots, grain, and fringing in the empty arrow and around the outside. Perfectly smooth crisp vector-like contours, solid flat fills: light mint #81E0B6 for the main R and teal #168B84 for the separate lower-left stroke. No texture or gradients. The large internal arrow cutout and all surrounding empty area must be completely transparent alpha with no colored remnants. Keep the bold compact shape and proportions. Center the mark in a square transparent canvas with an even 10 percent clear margin. Single logo, no text or extra artwork.
